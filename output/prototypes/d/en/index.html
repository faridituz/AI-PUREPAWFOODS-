PURE PAW / What every pack states

What every pack states

Facts taken from the current packaging of all seven SKUs — not marketing promises.

01

Full composition, with animal share

The label lists every ingredient; animal ingredients are stated as a share (40–51% depending on recipe).

02

Guaranteed analysis, printed per pack

Protein, fat, fibre, ash, moisture, calcium, phosphorus, taurine and chloride — with the measurement basis stated.

03

Feeding table and a 7-day transition

Daily amounts by body weight or age, plus a step-by-step 7-day switch from the previous food.

Product figures are stored once and shown identically in all languages.
